About Us

The story that started with Ahmed's dream

In Düsseldorf, Ahmed had €40,000 and a big dream.

He wanted to open a small restaurant. But he faced a wall of complexity: Gewerbeamt, IHK, Finanzamt, insurance, taxes, permits, rent...

He searched the internet for months. Visited 10 different websites. Talked to 5 consultants. Paid more than €3,000 on scattered consultations.

In the end, he discovered that 80% of founders fail in the first year — not because they lack ability, but because they don't find the right guidance at the right time.

PLANVOR was born here.

Our goal: no dream should be lost due to lack of information.

"Because you deserve to start your business with confidence, not confusion."
